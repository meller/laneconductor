# Spec: Product Hunt Listing

## Problem Statement
LaneConductor has strong initial traction from HackerNews and early adopter communities, but needs a structured launch on Product Hunt to reach a wider tech-savvy audience. Product Hunt launches require upfront preparation (relationships, assets, timing) to maximize impact and conversion to GitHub stars.

## Requirements

### Pre-Launch (Week 2 — 7 days before)
- REQ-1: Research and identify 3–5 active Product Hunt hunters in DevTools/Productivity category
- REQ-2: Build warm relationships with hunters (DM, share sneak preview, explain LaneConductor's value)
- REQ-3: Reach out to HackerNews/Reddit early adopters to request launch-day support
- REQ-4: Create polished PH listing draft with final tagline, description, and features list

### Launch Assets (Week 2)
- REQ-5: Record 60-90 second demo video (show Kanban dashboard in action, multi-repo workflow)
- REQ-6: Create 3–5 high-quality screenshots (dashboard, setup, multi-project view)
- REQ-7: Write compelling PH description (800–1000 words) with use cases, benefits, and differentiation
- REQ-8: Finalize tagline choice (poll internal team or early users if needed)

### Launch Day (Week 3)
- REQ-9: Schedule all launch-day communications (Twitter/X thread, Reddit thread, HN if eligible, Discord)
- REQ-10: Set up real-time monitoring (PH comment notifications, GitHub star rate, visitor analytics)
- REQ-11: Brief team and community on engagement expectations (respond to comments <2h)

### Post-Launch (Week 3+)
- REQ-12: Track metrics: PH upvotes, top-comment themes, GitHub stars gained, email signups
- REQ-13: Identify and address top 5 comment concerns (pain points, feature requests, objections)
- REQ-14: Prepare follow-up launch recap post (lessons learned, thank-you thread) for Day 2–3

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] PH listing is published and visible at producthunt.com
- [ ] Minimum 50 upvotes and top 5 ranking in DevTools category on launch day
- [ ] GitHub star rate increases >50% week-of launch
- [ ] Email signup list grows by 20+ new subscribers from PH traffic
- [ ] All top 5 launch-day comments are addressed within 24 hours
- [ ] Demo video is <90 seconds and shows core Kanban workflow clearly
- [ ] Tagline is chosen and locked 3 days before launch
- [ ] Hunters and early adopter community are briefed and committed to launch-day support

## Differentiation vs. Competitors
- **Local-first**: No cloud upload, full privacy (vs. other agent dashboards)
- **Multi-agent**: Run Claude + Gemini side-by-side (vs. single-agent tools)
- **Sovereign**: 100% self-hosted, zero cost (vs. SaaS solutions)
- **AI-native workflow**: Built for autonomous agents, not just humans (vs. generic project tools)

## Success Metrics
- **Engagement**: >100 upvotes, >50 comments by end of Day 1
- **Traffic**: >1000 GitHub visitors from PH link
- **Conversion**: >2% of visitors star the repo
- **Community**: >15 community members commit to launch-day support

## Launch Timeline
- **Day -7 (Week 2 Mon)**: Finalize assets, reach out to hunters
- **Day -3 (Week 2 Fri)**: Lock tagline, brief team/community
- **Day 0 (Week 3 Tue)**: Launch on Product Hunt, all-day engagement
- **Day +1 (Week 3 Wed)**: Monitor, respond, share recap thread
